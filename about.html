St. Marcellino Primary School

Giving every child a strong beginning

St. Marcellino Primary School is in Busiika Town Council, Luwero District. We welcome pupils from Nursery through Primary Seven (P.1–P.7).

Our teachers and parents work together to help pupils learn well, behave well, and believe in themselves.

Talk to our school

Our mission

We teach according to the Uganda National Curriculum and help our pupils become thoughtful, honest, and able learners.

Our approach

Our teachers create a supportive classroom where children can improve in their studies, make friends, and enjoy creative activities.

Our community

Parents and the people around Busiika are important partners in a child's education.
